## 🎯 学习目标

- 在社交/商务活动（networking event）中用英语自然地进行自我介绍
- 用英语询问和介绍职业（What do you do? / Where do you work?）
- 用英语讨论工作状态（用现在完成进行时 "How has your work been going?"）
- 掌握 "challenging", "demanding", "rewarding" 等工作相关核心形容词
- 学会用英语表达工作生活平衡（work-life balance）的讨论
- 用英语描述工作中遇到的挑战和解决方案

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、什么是 Networking Event

**Networking event** 指的是社交活动，人们在这些场合建立人脉、认识新朋友。

| 类型 | 英文 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| meetings | 会议 |
| parties | 派对 |
| forums | 论坛 |
| workshops | 工作坊/研讨会 |

> **老师的解释：** A networking event means a social event — like meetings, parties, forums, or workshops. These are the social events where you meet new people and build professional connections.

### 二、社交场合的自我介绍

#### 基本流程

```
A: Hi, my name is Christine.
B: Hi, my name is Jabin. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too. So, what do you do?
```

#### 关键句型

| 句型 | 用途 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| Hi, my name is... | 自我介绍 | Hi, my name is Jabin. |
| Nice to meet you. | 礼貌问候 | It's so nice to meet you. |
| What do you do? | 询问职业 | So, what do you do? |
| Where do you work? | 询问工作地点 | Where do you work? |
| Which company do you work for? | 询问公司 | Which company do you work for? |
| Do you like your job? | 询问工作满意度 | Do you like your job? |

### 三、职业名称与表达 (Jobs and Occupations)

#### 常见职业

| 英文 | 中文 | 工作内容 |
|------|------|----------|
| engineer | 工程师 | — |
| teacher | 老师 | teaching English classes |
| accountant | 会计师 | — |
| sales assistant | 销售助理 | — |
| manager | 经理 | manage the team working for the same goal |
| programmer | 程序员 | — |
| designer | 设计师 | — |
| architect | 建筑师 | design buildings |

#### 介绍职业的句型

```
I'm a programmer.                    →  我是一名程序员。
I'm an engineer.                     →  我是一名工程师。
I'm an architect.                    →  我是一名建筑师。

I work at an IT company.             →  我在一家IT公司工作。
I'm working for the Bank of Communications. →  我在交通银行工作。
I work in Shanghai.                  →  我在上海工作。
```

> **a vs an 的用法：** 以元音音素开头的单词前用 an（an engineer, an architect, an accountant），辅音音素开头用 a（a teacher, a manager, a programmer）。

### 四、讨论工作的核心句型

#### 询问工作进展 — 现在完成进行时

```
How has your work been going?
→ 你最近工作怎么样？
```

**回答模板：**

```
It's been really demanding, but also very rewarding.
→ 工作一直很繁重，但也很有成就感。

It's been a very challenging project, but also really rewarding.
→ 这是一个非常有挑战性的项目，但也很有收获。
```

#### 讨论工作满意度

```
Do you like your job?               →  你喜欢你的工作吗？
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.           →  是的，我喜欢。/ 不，我不喜欢。
I like it very much.                →  我非常喜欢。

Do you enjoy working there?         →  你喜欢在那里工作吗？
```

#### 讨论工作强度

```
How has your work been going?
→ It's been really demanding.       →  工作一直非常繁重。

Sometimes I feel my job is demanding.
→ 有时候我觉得我的工作很累人。
```

### 五、核心形容词辨析：Challenging vs Demanding vs Rewarding

这是本课的核心词汇组：

| 形容词 | 中文 | 含义 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **challenging** | 有挑战性的 | 需要努力才能完成，但通常是积极的 | It's been a very challenging project. |
| **demanding** | 要求高的/费力的 | 需要大量时间、精力和技能 | The job is really demanding. |
| **rewarding** | 有回报的/有成就感的 | 付出后有满足感和收获 | The job is challenging but also rewarding. |
| **tiring** | 累人的 | 让人感到疲劳 | Sometimes it can be a little bit tiring. |

**老师的解释：** "Demanding" means it requires you a lot of time, a lot of effort to do it. It's a demanding job. "Rewarding" means when you finish the work, you feel a sense of achievement — like you get the salary and feel the job is worthwhile.

#### 实用表达组合

```
It's challenging but rewarding.     →  有挑战性但有回报。
It's been really demanding.         →  一直非常繁重。
My job can be tiring sometimes.     →  我的工作有时很累人。
```

### 六、Meet the Deadline — 赶上截止日期

```
Have you been able to meet the deadline?
→ 你能按时完成吗？（赶上截止日期吗？）

It was too demanding, but yes, I met the deadline.
→ 工作太繁重了，但是的，我按时完成了。

We had to meet the deadline, but we needed more time.
→ 我们必须赶上截止日期，但我们需要更多时间。
```

### 七、工作生活平衡 (Work-Life Balance)

```
I think we should keep a balance between work and life.
→ 我觉得我们应该保持工作和生活的平衡。

It has a good balance between stress and relaxation.
→ 它在压力和放松之间有很好的平衡。

I can be busy for a while, but not for too long.
→ 我可以忙一阵子，但不能太久。

Maybe I can be busy for three or four days in a week,
and then I must have two or three days to have a really good rest without any work.
→ 也许我可以一周忙三四天，
  然后必须有两三天好好休息，完全不工作。

So I can refresh.
→ 这样我可以恢复精力。
```

### 八、放松方式 (Ways to Relax)

#### 讨论爱好和放松

```
What do you do to relax?
→ 你做什么来放松？

I do something that I want to do to relax myself.
→ 我做自己想做的事情来放松自己。

Such as doing some sports, playing the piano, and playing computer games.
→ 比如做运动、弹钢琴、玩电脑游戏。

Playing an instrument is a good way to relax.
→ 弹奏乐器是很好的放松方式。
```

#### 讨论学习乐器的经历

```
Have you been practicing the piano?
→ 你一直在练钢琴吗？

About one year.
→ 大概一年了。

Do you think it's difficult?
→ 你觉得难吗？

It was difficult at the beginning.
→ 一开始很难。

But as you practice more, you get more and more familiar with it.
→ 但随着练习增多，你会越来越熟悉。

It's like you conquer a difficult task.
→ 就像你征服了一个困难的任务。

That can really give us a sense of achievement.
→ 这真的能给我们带来成就感。
```

### 九、讨论工作中的挑战

#### 描述问题

```
What has been a challenging situation recently?
→ 最近有什么有挑战性的情况？

I have been having problems with the client recently.
They have been quite demanding.
→ 最近我和客户之间有些问题。他们的要求一直很高。

When I was working with the data they gave me,
I realized it wasn't right.
→ 当我处理他们给我的数据时，我发现数据不对。
```

#### 描述解决方案

```
We have agreed to work together to fix the data.
→ 我们已经同意一起合作修正数据。

So I think we will be able to meet the deadline.
→ 所以我认为我们能按时完成。

We need to find out some new technology tools to help with our work.
→ 我们需要找一些新的技术工具来帮助我们的工作。

Sometimes I need to think about faster, more effective ways to do my work.
→ 有时候我需要想一些更快、更有效的方法来做工作。
```

### 十、关于 AI 的讨论

```
Which AI do you use to help you solve problems at work?
→ 你用哪个AI来帮你解决工作中的问题？

I use AI to help me to summarize what I've learned at work.
→ 我用AI帮我总结工作中学到的东西。

I think it's wise to use AI to help with our study.
→ 我觉得用AI帮助学习是明智的。

We can use AI to solve some problems, not only at work,
but also some personal problems.
→ 我们可以用AI解决一些问题，不仅是工作上的，还有个人问题。

We can also use AI to help us to analyze or to come up with some plans.
→ 我们也可以用AI帮我们分析或制定一些计划。
```

### 十一、Yes/No 简短回答 (Short Answers)

| 问句 | 肯定回答 | 否定回答 |
|------|----------|----------|
| Do you like your job? | Yes, I do. | No, I don't. |
| Do you enjoy working there? | Yes, I do. | No, I don't. |
| Does he work every day? | Yes, he does. | No, he doesn't. |
| Have you met him? | Yes, I have. | No, I haven't. |

> **重点：** 助动词要与问句保持一致。Don't say just "Yes" or "No" — use the full short answer.

### 十二、完整对话模板

**At a Networking Event:**

```
A: Hi, my name is Christine.
B: Hi, my name is Jabin. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too. So, what do you do?
B: I'm a programmer. I work at an IT company in Shanghai.
A: Wow, that's interesting. Do you like your job?
B: Yes, I do. I like it very much. How about you?
A: I am a teacher. I basically teach English classes.
B: That's interesting. Do you like it?
A: Yes, I like it, but sometimes it can be a little bit tiring.
B: I understand. I think we should keep a balance between work and life.
A: I agree. How has your work been going recently?
B: It's been really demanding, but also very rewarding.
A: That's great to hear.
```
