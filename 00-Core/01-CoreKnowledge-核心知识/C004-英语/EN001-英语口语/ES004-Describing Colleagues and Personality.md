## 🎯 学习目标

- 区分 "What does he look like?"（外貌）和 "What's he like?"（性格）两种提问方式
- 用英语描述同事的外貌特征（身高、发型、眼睛颜色等）
- 用英语描述同事的性格特点（friendly, serious, hardworking, lazy 等）
- 掌握 "looks + 形容词" 与 "is + 形容词" 的使用差异
- 使用 "Have you met...?" 询问是否见过某人，用 "Not yet." 回答
- 正确使用介词搭配：friendly to、helpful to、talk to

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、两个核心提问句

| 问句 | 询问内容 | 回答方向 |
|------|----------|----------|
| **What does he look like?** | **外貌**（appearance） | tall, short hair, brown eyes... |
| **What's he like?** | **性格/个性**（personality） | friendly, serious, hardworking... |

**对话示例：**

```
A: Have you met our new colleague, Alex?
B: No, not yet. What's he like?
A: He's tall. He has brown eyes and black hair.

B: What's he like? (问性格)
A: He looks serious, but he's really nice and helpful.
```

> **记忆技巧**：
> - `look like` → 用眼睛能看到的 → 外貌
> - `be like` (is/are like) → 相处才能感受到的 → 性格

### 二、描述外貌 (Appearance)

#### 身高与体型

```
He's tall. / He's quite tall.    → 他很高。
He's short.                      → 他很矮。
He seems tall.                   → 他看起来很高。
He's shorter than me.            → 他比我矮。
```

#### 头发与眼睛

```
He has short black hair.         → 他有黑色短发。
He has blonde hair.              → 他有金色头发。
He has brown eyes.               → 他有棕色眼睛。
He has beautiful blue eyes.      → 他有漂亮的蓝眼睛。
```

### 三、描述性格 (Personality)

#### 核心形容词

| 形容词 | 中文 | 反义词 |
|--------|------|--------|
| friendly | 友好的 | unfriendly |
| kind | 善良的 | unkind |
| helpful | 乐于助人的 | unhelpful |
| hardworking | 勤奋的 | lazy |
| serious | 严肃的 | funny / easygoing |
| intelligent | 聪明的 | — |
| easygoing | 随和的 | serious / strict |
| nervous | 紧张的 | calm / relaxed |
| stressed | 有压力的 | relaxed |
| overworked | 过度劳累的 | — |
| happy | 开心的 | unhappy |

#### 描述句型

```
He's very friendly.              → 他很友好。
He looks serious.                → 他看起来很严肃。
He's really nice.                → 他真的很不错。
He's always busy.                → 他总是在忙。
She's very helpful to her colleagues. → 她对同事很乐于助人。
She's always smiling.            → 她总是在微笑。
```

### 四、"looks" vs "is" 的区别

| 表达 | 含义 | 使用场景 |
|------|------|----------|
| He looks serious. | 他**看起来**严肃 | 外表印象，实际可能不同 |
| He is serious. | 他**就是**严肃的 | 确认的性格特点 |

**典型用法：**

```
He looks unfriendly, but when I met him, he's really nice.
→ 他看起来不友好，但当我认识他之后，他其实人很好。

At first, he looks lazy to me.
But in fact, he is a really hardworking person.
→ 一开始我觉得他看起来很懒。
  但实际上他是一个非常勤奋的人。
```

> 这是非常地道的表达模式：**Looks + 形容词... but in fact + 相反的 is + 形容词**，用来描述"第一印象 ≠ 实际性格"。

### 五、"looks like" 的另一种用法

`looks like` 后面可以接句子，表示推测：

```
He looks like he's always busy.     → 他看起来好像总是在忙。
She looks like she's friendly.      → 她看起来好像很友好。
It looks like he's stressed.        → 看起来他压力很大。
```

### 六、核心介词搭配

英语中这些形容词后面需要搭配特定介词：

| 搭配 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| **friendly to** sb | My boss is friendly to us. |
| **helpful to** sb | She's very helpful to the team. |
| **talk to** sb | She never talks to me. |
| **work with** sb | I work with her. |

```
❌ She is friendly with me.
✅ She is friendly to me.

❌ She talked with me.
✅ She talked to me.
```

> 口语中 `talk with` 也可以接受（强调双向交流），但 `talk to` 更常用。

### 七、询问和介绍新同事的对话

#### 关键句型

```
Have you met our new colleague, Alex?
→ 你见过我们的新同事 Alex 了吗？

No, not yet. What's he like?
→ 还没呢。他怎么样？

What does he look like?
→ 他长什么样？

Can you tell me about him?
→ 你能跟我讲讲他吗？
```

#### "Have you met...?" 的完整回答

```
Have you met our new boss?
✅ Yes, I have.
✅ No, I haven't.
✅ No, not yet.          ← 最自然的否定回答
```

### 八、安排见面的表达

```
We can arrange a dinner together.   → 我们可以安排一起吃个饭。
We can arrange a meeting soon.      → 我们可以尽快安排一次会面。
We can meet next time.              → 我们下次可以见面。
I'd like to meet him.               → 我想见见他。
I would like to meet him soon.      → 我想尽快见到他。
```

### 九、"outside of work" vs "when it comes to work"

描述一个人在工作内外有不同的表现：

```
When it comes to work, he could be really serious.
→ 涉及到工作时，他可能很严肃。

Outside of work, he's friendly and easygoing.
→ 工作之外，他友好又随和。
```

### 十、完整对话模板

**Talking About a New Colleague:**

```
A: Hi, have you met our new colleague, Alex?
B: No, not yet. What's he like?

A: He's tall. He has short black hair and brown eyes.
   He looks serious, but he's really nice and helpful.
B: Is he hardworking?

A: Yes, he's very hardworking. He's always busy.
   But he's friendly to everyone on the team.
B: That sounds great! I'd like to meet him.

A: We can arrange a meeting next time.
   I think you would like him.
B: Great. Thank you for telling me about him.
```

**Describing a Boss:**

```
A: Have you met our new boss?
B: Yes, I have.

A: What's he like? Is he serious?
B: Yes, he looks serious and intelligent.
   When it comes to work, he's really serious.
   But outside of work, he's friendly and easygoing.
   He's a good friend.

A: What does he look like?
B: He has short hair and he's not very tall.
   He's shorter than me.
```

### 十一、应对压力的表达

```
Do you sometimes feel overworked?       → 你有时会感到过度劳累吗？
How do you cope with that?              → 你怎么应对？

I do sports.                            → 我做运动。
I listen to music.                      → 我听音乐。
Doing exercises and listening to music   → 锻炼和听音乐
can help us relieve the pressure.        可以帮助我们缓解压力。
```
