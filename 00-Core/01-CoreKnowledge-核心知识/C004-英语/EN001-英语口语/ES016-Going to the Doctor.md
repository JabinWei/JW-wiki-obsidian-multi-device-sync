## 🎯 学习目标

- 用英语描述常见病症症状（headache, sore throat, fever, cough, flu, runny nose）
- 在医生问诊场景中用英语描述自己的症状
- 用 "I'm sorry to hear that" 对他人表达同理心
- 用 You should / You shouldn't 给出健康建议
- 区分 "record" 和 "take notes" 的用法
- 掌握看病场景的核心句型：What seems to be the problem? / What should I do?

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、常见症状词汇

| 症状 | 英文 | 相关表达 |
|------|------|----------|
| 头痛 | headache | My head hurts. / I have a headache. |
| 喉咙痛 | sore throat | I have a sore throat. |
| 发烧 | fever | I have a fever. / My head feels so hot. |
| 咳嗽 | cough | I have a cough. / I started coughing three days ago. |
| 流感 | flu | I think I have the flu. |
| 流鼻涕 | runny nose | I have a runny nose. |
| 头晕 | dizzy | I feel dizzy. |
| 胃痛 | stomachache | I have a stomachache. |
| 生病 | sick / ill | I'm feeling sick. / I'm ill. |

> **"a" 的使用规则**：大多数症状前面用 `a`——`a headache`、`a fever`、`a cough`、`a sore throat`
>
> 老师说："Whatever sickness you have, it always comes with 'a'. You don't have two heads!"

### 二、描述症状的句型

#### 基础句型

```
I have a headache.            → 我头痛。
I have a sore throat.         → 我喉咙痛。
I have a fever.               → 我发烧了。
I have a cough.               → 我咳嗽。
```

#### 更详细的描述

```
My head hurts.                → 我头疼。
My head feels so hot.         → 我的头感觉很烫。
I feel dizzy.                 → 我感觉头晕。
I feel both hot and cold.     → 我感觉又热又冷。
My body is cold but my head is hot. → 身体冷但头热。
I started coughing three days ago.  → 我三天前开始咳嗽。
I've been coughing for three days.  → 我已经咳嗽了三天。
```

#### 错误表达注意

```
❌ My throat is ill.              → 身体部位不能用 ill
✅ I have a sore throat.          → 正确
❌ My head is ill.                → 不正确
✅ My head hurts. / I have a headache. → 正确
```

### 三、表达同理心

当别人告诉你他们生病了，用这些表达回应：

| 表达 | 中文 | 使用场景 |
|------|------|----------|
| I'm sorry to hear that. | 听到这个很难过。 | 任何坏消息 |
| That's too bad. | 太糟糕了。 | 不如意的消息 |
| Get better soon. | 早日康复。 | 专用的生病祝福 |
| I hope you feel better. | 希望你好起来。 | 生病/不舒服 |

> 老师说："These phrases you can use not only for hearing someone who is sick, but also when someone tells you bad news."

### 四、给出建议：You should / You shouldn't

| 建议 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 你应该多休息 | You should get some rest. |
| 你应该吃些药 | You should take some medicine. |
| 你应该待在家 | You should stay at home. |
| 你应该喝大量液体 | You should drink lots of liquids. |
| 你不应该去上班 | You shouldn't go to work. |
| 你不应该喝冰水 | You shouldn't drink iced water. |

**对话示例：**

```
A: I have a headache and a sore throat.
B: I'm sorry to hear that. You shouldn't go to work.
   You should get some rest and take some medicine.
A: Thanks. I think I'll stay at home today.
```

### 五、医生问诊场景 — 核心句型

#### 医生常用句

```
What seems to be the problem?    → 有什么问题？
What are your symptoms?          → 你有哪些症状？
Any other symptoms?              → 还有其他症状吗？
Have you taken your temperature? → 你量体温了吗？
Any fever?                       → 发烧吗？
```

#### 病人常用句

```
I feel sick.                     → 我生病了。
I have a fever and a cough.      → 我发烧并且咳嗽。
What should I do?                → 我该怎么办？
Should I take some medicine?     → 我应该吃药吗？
Can I have the prescription?     → 能给我处方吗？
```

#### 医生给出建议

```
I'll prescribe something for the symptoms.  → 我会开一些缓解症状的药。
You need to get plenty of rest.             → 你需要充分休息。
Be sure to drink lots of liquids.           → 一定要多喝液体。
Come back if you're not feeling better in a few days. → 如果几天后还没好转，再回来。
```

### 六、完整问诊对话

```
Doctor: Hello. What seems to be the problem?

Patient: I feel sick. My head is so hot, and sometimes I feel cold.
         I also have a cough. I started coughing three days ago.

Doctor: I see. Any other symptoms? Any fever?

Patient: I'm not sure. I haven't taken my temperature yet.

Doctor: Well, based on the symptoms you described,
        it sounds like you have the flu.

Patient: What should I do?

Doctor: You need to get plenty of rest and drink lots of liquids.
        I'll prescribe some medicine for the symptoms.
        Come back if you're not feeling better in a few days.

Patient: Can I have the prescription?

Doctor: Sure. Go downstairs and you can get the medicine at the counter.

Patient: Thank you very much.
```

### 七、实用词汇辨析

#### record vs take notes

| 词 | 含义 | 场景 |
|----|------|------|
| **record** | 录音/录像 | 用手机/相机录制 |
| **take notes** | 做笔记 | 用纸笔记录要点 |

```
The doctor is taking notes.       ✅ 
The doctor is recording.          ❌ （除非真的在录音）
The class is being recorded.      ✅
```

#### clinic vs hospital

| 词 | 中文 | 规模 |
|----|------|------|
| **clinic** | 诊所 | 小型社区医疗机构 |
| **hospital** | 医院 | 大型综合医院 |

#### prescription

```
prescription    → 处方（医生开的药方）
medicine list   → 药品清单
pharmacy        → 药房（医院外）
counter         → 柜台（医院内取药处）
```

### 八、重要语法点

#### 现在完成进行时 — 描述持续的症状

```
I started coughing three days ago.     → 三天前开始咳嗽（一般过去时）
I've been coughing for three days.     → 已经咳嗽三天了（现在完成进行时，强调持续）
```

> 现在完成进行时 = have/has been + 动词-ing，用于描述从过去持续到现在的状态/动作。

#### pay attention to

```
❌ What should I be attention to?
✅ What should I pay attention to?

❌ Be attention!
✅ Pay attention!
```

### 九、健康相关的日常表达

```
I don't get sick too often.           → 我不常生病。
You look very hot. Are you feeling sick? → 你看起来很烫，你生病了吗？
I did a COVID test. Don't worry.       → 我做了新冠检测。别担心。
Are you sure it's not COVID?           → 你确定不是新冠吗？
Stay away from seafood.                → 远离海鲜。（中式建议）
```
