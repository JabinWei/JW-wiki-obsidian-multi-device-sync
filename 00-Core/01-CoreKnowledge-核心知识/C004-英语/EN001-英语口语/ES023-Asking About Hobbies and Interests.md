## 🎯 学习目标

- 用英语自然地询问他人的兴趣爱好
- 用 "What do you like to do in your free time?" 开启兴趣话题
- 学会烘焙 (baking) 相关词汇：roll the dough, bake, baking
- 用 "rock and roll" 的类比理解 roll 的含义
- 用 "Are you a... person?" 询问对方类型偏好

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、询问他人兴趣的多种方式

| 问句 | 场景 |
|------|------|
| What do you like to do in your free time? | 通用询问 |
| What are your hobbies? | 直接问爱好 |
| What do you do for fun? | 更口语 |
| Are you interested in...? | 针对具体活动 |
| Are you a... person? | 问偏好类型 |

**"Are you a... person?" 句型：**

```
Are you a dog person or a cat person?
→ 你是喜欢狗的人还是喜欢猫的人？

Are you a coffee person?
→ 你是喝咖啡的人吗？

Are you an outdoor person?
→ 你是喜欢户外活动的人吗？

Are you a morning person?
→ 你是早起的人吗？
```

### 二、烘焙 (Baking) 相关词汇

```
baking                → 烘焙
bake                  → 烘焙（动词）
roll the dough        → 擀面团
dough                 → 面团
bread                 → 面包
dessert               → 甜点
make bread            → 做面包
```

**动作描述：**

```
Roll the dough — this is how you make bread or make desserts.
→ 擀面团 — 这就是做面包或甜点的方法。

Sometimes you can call it "doing some baking."
→ 有时你可以称之为"做烘焙"。
```

### 三、"Rock and Roll" 类比

老师用 "rock and roll" 帮助学生理解 "roll"：

```
rock and roll  → 摇滚乐（roll 是"滚动"）
roll the dough → 擀面团（roll 也是"滚动"的动作）
```

> 生活中的 "roll" 动作：rolling a ball（滚球）、rolling your eyes（翻白眼）

### 四、询问兴趣的完整对话

**Asking About Free Time：**

```
A: What do you like to do in your free time?
B: I like listening to music and playing basketball.

A: Are you a sports person?
B: Yeah, I guess so. I play basketball every weekend.

A: What about baking? Do you like cooking or baking?
B: Not really. I'm not good at it. But I like eating!
```

**Asking About Hobbies：**

```
A: Do you have any hobbies?
B: Yes, I have a few. I like photography and I collect stamps.

A: Wow, stamp collecting! That's interesting.
   How long have you been doing that?

B: For about three years. My grandfather started me on it.

A: That's cool. Are you good at photography too?

B: I'm interested in it, but I'm not very good at it yet.
   I'm still learning.
```

### 五、问与答中的实用表达

#### 回答不想回答的问题

```
Not really.     → 不太是。
I guess so.     → 算是吧。
It depends.     → 看情况。
Not my thing.   → 不是我的菜。
```

#### 表达好奇

```
That's interesting!          → 那很有趣！
Tell me more about it.       → 多跟我说说。
How did you get into that?   → 你是怎么开始做那个的？
How long have you been doing that? → 你做这个多久了？
```

### 六、关于 "baking" 的发音

```
bake    /beɪk/       → 烘烤
baking  /ˈbeɪkɪŋ/    → 烘焙
bread   /bred/       → 面包
dough   /dəʊ/        → 面团（gh 不发音）
dessert /dɪˈzɜːt/    → 甜点（注意和 desert /ˈdezət/ "沙漠"区分）
```

### 七、课中纠正的关键错误

#### "I hope that I'll be cooking" — 表达想学会的愿望

当想说"我希望学会做某件事"：

```
❌ I hope that I'll be cooking.     → 听起来很别扭
✅ I'd like to learn cooking.       → 我想学烹饪。
✅ I want to learn how to cook.     → 同上
✅ I hope to get better at cooking. → 我希望烹饪能做得更好。
```

#### 关于缺席课程的道歉

```
I missed the class last week.
→ 我上周缺课了。

I forgot about the lesson.
→ 我忘记上课了。

I got the notification wrong.
→ 我看错了通知。
```
