## 🎯 学习目标
- 掌握办公室常见物品和区域的英语词汇
- 使用 There is / There are 句型描述办公室设施的位置
- 使用量词（quantifiers）表达数量的多少：a few, some, a lot of, many
- 学会用方位介词（near, behind, in front of, on the right）描述位置
- 练习带新同事参观办公室的角色扮演对话
- 掌握常用的礼貌回应表达（You're welcome / It's my pleasure / Pardon 等）

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、办公室词汇（Office Vocabulary）

**房间/区域/地点**
- reception / reception area — 前台 / 接待区
- meeting room — 会议室
- kitchen — 厨房
- restroom — 洗手间（比 WC 更正式自然的说法）
- elevator — 电梯
- stairs — 楼梯
- office — 办公室
- front desk — 前台

**物品/设施**
- desk / chair — 桌子 / 椅子
- computer — 电脑
- printer — 打印机
- coffee machine — 咖啡机
- coffee cup / mug — 咖啡杯 / 马克杯
- water cooler — 饮水机
- plant — 植物
- colleague — 同事

### 二、There is / There are 句型

**核心规则**
- There is + 单数 / 不可数名词：There is a coffee machine in the kitchen.
- There are + 复数名词：There are some chairs in the office.
- There is no + 名词（否定）：There is no water in the cooler.
- 不可数名词（如 water）不能用复数，要说 "There is water"，不能说 "There are waters"

### 三、量词（Quantifiers）

- a few + 复数名词 — 几个（数量少）：There are a few computers in the office.
- some + 复数名词 — 一些：There are some chairs over here.
- a lot of / lots of + 复数名词 — 很多：There are a lot of coffee cups in the kitchen.
- many + 复数名词 — 许多：There are many desks here.
- no + 名词 — 没有：There is no water.

> both 只用于两个事物；三个及以上用 all，不要混淆。

### 四、方位表达（Locations & Prepositions）

**位置词**
- over here — 在这边（靠近说话者）
- over there — 在那边（远离说话者）
- on the right — 在右边
- on the left — 在左边

**介词短语**
- in front of — 在...前面
- behind — 在...后面（不要说 "behind of"，behind 后面直接加名词）
- near — 在...附近（不要说 "near with"，near 直接加名词）
- close to — 靠近

**例句**
- There is a meeting room on the right.
- The restroom is near the manager's office.
- The manager's office is in front of the restroom.
- There are some restrooms in different places.

### 五、礼貌表达与回应（Polite Expressions）

**回应感谢**
- You're welcome.
- It's my pleasure. / My pleasure.
- No problem.

**没听清/请对方重复**
- Sorry? / Excuse me?
- Pardon?
- Please say it again.（不能说 Please again，again 不是动词）

**回应称赞/评论**
- It's such a big office! → Yeah, we have many desks and computers here.
- 对于陈述句（非问句），可以直接附和或补充说明，不需要强行用句子回应

### 六、语法纠正重点

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| near with the manager | near the manager | near 是介词，后面直接加名词 |
| behind of the restroom | behind the restroom | behind 后面直接加名词 |
| There are waters | There is water | water 不可数 |
| The area is kitchen | The area is the kitchen | 特定区域要加 the |
| both (用于三个以上) | all | both 仅用于两者 |
| Please again | Please say it again | again 不是动词 |
