## 🎯 学习目标

- 用英语询问和描述人物的外貌特征（What does he/she look like?）
- 用英语询问和描述人物的性格特征（What's he/she like?）
- 区分 "looks"（看起来）与 "is"（事实上）的用法差异
- 掌握 "looks like" 与 "looks + 形容词" 的语法区别
- 学会用 "at first... but in fact..." 表达外表与实际的对比
- 用英语介绍新同事并描述其外貌、性格和工作

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、询问人物外貌 vs 性格

英语中询问外貌和性格使用不同的句型：

| 询问内容 | 句型 | 回答方向 |
|----------|------|----------|
| **外貌** | **What does he/she look like?** | 身高、体型、头发、眼睛等 |
| **性格** | **What's he/she like?** | 友好、严肃、勤奋、聪明等 |

**例句：**
- A: Have you met our new colleague, Alex?
- B: No, not yet. What's he like?
- A: He's intelligent and really nice. He looks serious, but he's actually very friendly.

### 二、描述外貌 (Describing Appearance)

#### 核心句型

```
What does he/she look like?
→ He/She has + 形容词 + 名词.
→ He/She is + 形容词.
```

#### 外貌词汇

| 类别 | 词汇 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 身高 | tall, short, quite tall, medium height | He's quite tall. / He's shorter than me. |
| 头发 | short/long, black/brown/blonde/curly/straight hair | He has short black hair. / She has long brown hair. |
| 眼睛 | blue/brown/green eyes | He has beautiful blue eyes. / She has brown eyes. |
| 其他 | wear glasses, beard, moustache | She wears glasses. |
| 比较 | taller than, shorter than | He's shorter than me about 10 cm. |

#### 常见错误

```
✅ He has short black hair.        （形容词顺序：长短 + 颜色 + 名词）
❌ He has a short black hair.      （hair 不可数，不加 a）
✅ He has brown hair.
✅ He is tall. / He looks tall.
```

### 三、描述性格 (Describing Personality)

#### 核心句型

```
What's he/she like?
→ He/She is + 形容词.
→ He/She looks + 形容词.        （看起来...）
```

#### 性格词汇

| 正面性格 | 中文 | 负面/中性 | 中文 |
|----------|------|-----------|------|
| friendly | 友好的 | serious | 严肃的 |
| kind | 善良的 | lazy | 懒惰的 |
| helpful | 乐于助人的 | nervous | 紧张的 |
| intelligent | 聪明的 | stressed | 有压力的 |
| hardworking | 勤奋的 | overworked | 过度劳累的 |
| easygoing | 随和的 | quiet | 安静的 |
| funny | 有趣的 | — | — |
| happy | 开心的 | — | — |
| nice | 好的/友善的 | — | — |
| smiley | 爱笑的 | — | — |

### 四、"Looks" vs "Is" — 外表印象 vs 事实

这是本课的核心语法点：

| 表达 | 含义 | 侧重点 |
|------|------|--------|
| **He is + adj.** | 他（事实上）是... | 陈述已知事实 |
| **He looks + adj.** | 他看起来... | 基于外表的印象/推测 |

**对比练习：**

```
He looks serious.        →  他看起来很严肃。（外表印象，不一定真是）
He is serious.           →  他很严肃。（事实如此）

She looks friendly.      →  她看起来很友好。
She is friendly.         →  她很友好。

He looks lazy to me.     →  在我看来他看起来很懒。
But in fact, he is a really hardworking person.  →  但事实上，他是个非常勤奋的人。
```

> **关键区别：** "looks + 形容词" 表达的是视觉印象，可能与事实不符。这正是后续用 "but in fact..." 做转折的基础。

### 五、"Looks Like" vs "Looks + 形容词" — 语法辨析

这是学生常犯的错误：

| 语法结构 | 后面接 | 例句 |
|----------|--------|------|
| **looks + 形容词** | 形容词 | He looks serious. |
| **looks like + 名词** | 名词/名词短语 | He looks like a serious person. |

**常见错误：**

```
❌ He looks like serious.         （like 后不能直接接形容词）
✅ He looks serious.              （looks + 形容词）
✅ He looks like a serious person.（looks like + 名词短语）
```

### 六、介词搭配：Friendly/Helpful + to

| 搭配 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| **be friendly to** sb | She is friendly to everyone. |
| **be helpful to** sb | He's always helpful to me. |
| **talk to** sb | She never talked to me. |

```
✅ My boss is friendly to us.
✅ My teacher is very helpful to me.
❌ She friendly with you.  →  ✅ She is friendly to you.
```

### 七、"At First... But in Fact..." — 外表与实际的对比

这是一个非常地道的表达结构，用于描述第一印象与实际情况的差异：

```
At first, he looks lazy to me.
But in fact, he is a really hardworking person.

→ 一开始，我觉得他看起来很懒。
  但事实上，他是个非常勤奋的人。
```

**类似表达：**
- He looks serious, but he's actually very friendly.
- She seems quiet, but in fact, she's very funny.
- He looks unfriendly, but when I met him, he's really nice.

### 八、"When It Comes to Work" — 在...方面

这是一个高频口语表达，用于限定讨论的范围：

```
When it comes to work, he could be really serious.
→ 在工作方面，他可能非常严肃。

But outside of work, he's friendly and easygoing.
→ 但在工作之外，他很友好、随和。
```

### 九、工作职业 (Jobs and Occupations)

#### 询问职业

```
What do you do?              →  你是做什么工作的？
What do you do for work?     →  你的工作是什么？
```

#### 职业词汇

| 英文 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| professor | 教授 |
| accountant | 会计师 |
| sales assistant | 销售助理 |
| scientist | 科学家 |
| manager | 经理 |
| designer | 设计师 |
| programmer | 程序员 |
| leader of a team/department | 团队/部门负责人 |

#### 讨论工作

```
What is the best part of your job?
→ 你工作中最棒的部分是什么？

I enjoy being a programmer.
→ 我喜欢做程序员。

I get a sense of achievement when I finish my work.
→ 当我完成工作时，我会有成就感。

The best part is the thinking process.
→ 最棒的部分是思考的过程。
```

### 十、实用口语表达

| 表达 | 中文 | 使用场景 |
|------|------|----------|
| Have you met...? | 你见过...吗？ | 引出新同事话题 |
| No, not yet. | 还没有。 | 回答 Have you... 问句 |
| What's he/she like? | 他/她是个什么样的人？ | 询问性格 |
| What does he/she look like? | 他/她长什么样？ | 询问外貌 |
| He looks... to me. | 在我看来他看起来... | 表达个人印象 |
| I think so. | 我也这样认为。 | 表示赞同 |
| That sounds great. | 听起来不错。 | 回应好消息 |
| Thank you for telling me about him. | 谢谢你告诉我关于他的事。 | 礼貌结束话题 |
| I would like to meet him. | 我想见见他。 | 表达意愿 |
| We can arrange a meeting soon. | 我们可以尽快安排见面。 | 提议行动 |
| leave a good impression on sb | 给某人留下好印象 | 职场/社交 |
| It seems that... | 似乎... | 表达推测 |

### 十一、课程对话模板

**Introducing a New Colleague（介绍新同事）：**

```
A: Hi, have you met our new colleague, Alex?
B: No, not yet. What's he like?
A: He's intelligent and really nice. He's always smiley and very helpful to the team.
B: That sounds great. What does he look like?
A: He has blonde hair and beautiful blue eyes. He's quite tall.
B: Thank you for telling me about him. I would like to meet him.
A: We can arrange a dinner together next time.
```

**Describing the New Boss（描述新老板）：**

```
A: Have you met our new boss?
B: Yes, I have.
A: What's he like? Is he serious?
B: He looks serious, and he's intelligent. But he's also friendly to us.
   When it comes to work, he could be really serious.
   But outside of work, he's friendly and easygoing.
A: It seems that he's a great leader.
B: Yes, I think so too.
```
