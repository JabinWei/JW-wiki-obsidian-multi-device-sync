## 🎯 学习目标

- 用 "good at / bad at / interested in" 描述能力和兴趣
- 用 "I collect..." 描述收藏爱好
- 用 "I'm on a... team / I belong to a... club" 描述团队/俱乐部参与
- 用 "What do you like to do in your free time?" 询问他人爱好
- 正确发音 photography（重音在第二音节）和 "I'm not good at it" 的连读
- 区分 "cost"（花钱）和 "spend/take"（花时间）

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、描述能力的三大核心短语

| 短语 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **good at** | 擅长的 | I'm good at running. |
| **bad at** | 不擅长的 | I'm bad at cooking. |
| **interested in** | 感兴趣的 | I'm interested in photography. |

**注意介词**：
```
✅ good at + 名词/动词-ing
✅ bad at + 名词/动词-ing
✅ interested in + 名词/动词-ing

❌ good in / interested at
```

**语法注意**：`good at` 和 `bad at` 后面接动词时用 **-ing** 形式：
```
I'm good at playing tennis.     ✅
I'm good at play tennis.        ❌
```

### 二、"I'm not good at it" — 连读技巧

```
单独读：    I'm  not  good  at  it.
连读：      I'm no(t) goo(d) a(t) i(t).
听起来像：  I'm no-goo-da-dit
```

> 老师说：连读不是把一个一个的词切开来读，而是像流水一样连在一起。不需要用力读每个辅音结尾。

### 三、描述爱好的多样句型

#### 基础句型

```
I like listening to music.             → 我喜欢听音乐。
I love playing computer games.         → 我热爱玩电脑游戏。
I'm interested in photography.         → 我对摄影感兴趣。
```

#### 进阶句型（Level 3+）

```
I belong to a book club.               → 我加入了一个读书俱乐部。
I'm on a tennis team.                  → 我参加了一个网球队。
I collect stamps and coins.            → 我收集邮票和硬币。
I like getting together with friends.  → 我喜欢和朋友聚会。
```

> **Level 3 的特色**：用 `belong to / on a team / collect` 替代简单的 `I like`，让表达更丰富。

### 四、收藏类爱好

```
I collect stamps.        → 我收集邮票。
I collect coins.         → 我收集硬币。
I collect comics.        → 我收集漫画。
```

**stamp（邮票）相关词汇：**

```
stamp     → 邮票
envelope  → 信封
letter    → 信
address   → 地址
mail      → 邮寄
```

### 五、询问爱好的方式

```
What do you like to do in your free time?
→ 你空余时间喜欢做什么？

What are your hobbies?
→ 你的爱好是什么？

What are you interested in?
→ 你对什么感兴趣？

Are you on any teams?
→ 你参加了什么团队吗？

What do you collect?
→ 你收集什么？
```

### 六、photography 发音

```
photo     /ˈfəʊtəʊ/    重音第一音节
photograph /ˈfəʊtəɡrɑːf/ 重音第一音节
photography /fəˈtɒɡrəfi/ 重音第二音节 ⚠️
```

> 重音从第一音节 **移到了第二音节**。不要读成 "PHO-to-gra-phy"

### 七、"be good at" vs "be bad at" 的语用

```
✅ I'm not good at cooking.    → 中性，只是陈述不擅长
✅ I'm bad at cooking.         → 语法正确，但语气更强

✅ I'm not very good at it yet. → 我不太擅长—还（暗示还在学习）
```

> **老师说**："I'm bad at it" 语法正确但听起来有些奇怪。用 "I'm not good at it" 或 "I'm not very good at it yet" 更自然。"yet" 暗示你还在努力，不是永远不擅长。

### 八、"cost" vs "spend" vs "take" 深入

```
✅ It costs a lot of money.        (cost = 花钱)
❌ It costs much time.             (cost 不能用于时间)
✅ It takes a lot of time.         (take = 花时间)
✅ I spend a lot of time on it.    (spend = 人作主语)
```

### 九、完整对话

**What do you like to do in your free time?**

```
A: What do you like to do in your free time?

B: Well, there are many things I'm interested in.
   I like listening to music, playing computer games,
   and I'm on a tennis team. I practice every weekend.

A: Wow, you're busy. What are you good at?

B: I'm good at tennis and computer games.
   But I'm not very good at cooking yet.
   Sometimes I cook for my friends, but it doesn't always taste good!

A: What about collecting? Do you collect anything?

B: Not really. I think it takes too much time.
   I prefer doing active things.

A: What about photography?

B: I'm interested in photography, but I'm not good at it.
   I'd like to learn more about it.
```

### 十、"I'm scared of..." 表达恐惧

```
I'm scared of heights.                  → 我恐高。
I'm scared to stand in high places.     → 我害怕站在高处。
Climbing mountains is difficult for me because I'm scared of heights.
→ 爬山对我来说很难，因为我恐高。
```
