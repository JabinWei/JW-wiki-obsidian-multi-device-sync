## 🎯 学习目标

- 用英语说出常见食物的名称和来源国家
- 用英语描述食物的味道（fresh, salty, sweet, sour, spicy, tasty, delicious）
- 用 "What's the food like?" 询问食物怎么样
- 用 "Let's have..." / "How about..." / "Shall we..." 提议午餐吃什么
- 正确使用国家名 vs 国籍形容词（Italy/Italian, Japan/Japanese, Mexico/Mexican 等）

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、国际美食词汇

| 食物 | 中文 | 来源国家 | 国籍形容词 |
|------|------|----------|------------|
| pasta | 意大利面 | Italy | Italian |
| sushi | 寿司 | Japan | Japanese |
| curry | 咖喱 | India | Indian |
| tacos / taco | 墨西哥卷饼 | Mexico | Mexican |
| dumplings | 饺子 | China | Chinese |

**句型：**

```
Pasta is from Italy. It's Italian food.
→ 意大利面来自意大利。它是意大利食物。

Sushi is from Japan. It's Japanese food.
→ 寿司来自日本。它是日本食物。

Curry is from India. It's Indian food.
→ 咖喱来自印度。它是印度食物。

Tacos are from Mexico. It's Mexican food.
→ 墨西哥卷饼来自墨西哥。它是墨西哥食物。
```

> **注意区分**：
> - 国家名：Italy, Japan, India, Mexico, China
> - 国籍形容词：Italian, Japanese, Indian, Mexican, Chinese
> - 说"来自某国"用 from + 国家名：from Italy
> - 说"某国食物"用国籍形容词：Italian food

### 三、描述食物味道的形容词

| 形容词 | 中文 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| **fresh** | 新鲜的 | The salad is fresh. |
| **salty** | 咸的 | The soup is salty. |
| **sweet** | 甜的 | The cake is sweet. |
| **sour** | 酸的 | The lemon is sour. |
| **spicy** | 辣的 | The curry is spicy. |
| **tasty** | 好吃的 | The pasta is tasty. |
| **delicious** | 美味的 | The sushi is delicious. |
| **crispy** | 脆的 | The bread is crispy. |

#### 询问食物味道

```
What's the salad like?      → 沙拉怎么样？
It's fresh.                 → 很新鲜。

What's the soup like?       → 汤怎么样？
It's salty and sour.        → 又咸又酸。

What's the pasta like?      → 意大利面怎么样？
It's salty. Maybe a little spicy. → 咸的，也许有点辣。
```

### 四、表达喜欢和不喜欢的食物

#### 喜欢

```
I like pasta.              → 我喜欢意大利面。
I love sushi.              → 我爱寿司。
I'm a fan of Italian food. → 我是意大利食物的粉丝。
My favorite food is...     → 我最喜欢的食物是…
```

#### 不喜欢

```
I don't like spicy food.      → 我不喜欢辣的食物。
I'm not a fan of curry.       → 我不太喜欢咖喱。
I don't like it.              → 我不喜欢它。
```

### 五、提议午餐吃什么

#### 四大提议句型

| 句型 | 语气 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **Let's have...** | 直接提议 | Let's have pasta for lunch. |
| **How about...** | 建议 | How about sushi? |
| **Shall we...** | 较正式 | Shall we have Chinese food? |
| **What about...** | 替代建议 | What about salad? |

**对话示例：**

```
A: What do you want for lunch?
B: I don't know. What about you?

A: How about pasta? I know a good Italian restaurant.
B: Hmm, I had pasta yesterday. Let's have something different.

A: Shall we have sushi then?
B: That sounds great! I'm a fan of Japanese food.

A: Okay, let's have sushi for lunch.
```


### 七、讨论一日三餐

```
breakfast      → 早餐
lunch          → 午餐
dinner         → 晚餐

What do you usually have for breakfast?
→ 你早餐通常吃什么？

What shall we have for lunch?
→ 我们午餐吃什么？

Have you eaten dinner?
→ 你吃晚饭了吗？
No, not yet.
→ 还没。
```

### 八、Taste vs Flavor

| 词 | 中文 | 用法 |
|----|------|------|
| **taste** | 味道（名词/动词） | It has a sweet taste. / Taste it! |
| **tasty** | 好吃的（形容词） | The soup is tasty. |
| **flavor** | 风味/口味 | What flavor do you like? |

```
This dish has a really good flavor.  → 这道菜风味很好。
The soup is very tasty.              → 汤很好喝。
```

### 九、常见语法纠正

#### 国籍 vs 国家名

```
❌ It's from Mexican.
✅ It's from Mexico. / It's Mexican food.

❌ from Italian
✅ from Italy / Italian food
```

> **规则**：`from + 国家名`（名词），`国籍形容词 + food`（形容词修饰名词）

#### "a" 与可数/不可数

```
✅ I like pasta.            (pasta 不可数，不加 a)
✅ I had pasta yesterday.   (不可数)
✅ I like tacos.            (taco 可数，加 s)
```

### 十、完整对话模板

**Deciding What to Have for Lunch:**

```
A: I'm hungry. What shall we have for lunch?
B: I don't know. What do you feel like eating?

A: How about Indian food? I love curry.
B: Hmm, I'm not a big fan of spicy food.

A: What about Japanese food? Sushi is not spicy at all.
B: That's a good idea! I love sushi.

A: Let's have sushi then. There's a good Japanese restaurant near here.
B: Sounds great! What's their sushi like?

A: It's very fresh and tasty. You'll like it.
B: Okay, let's go!
```

**Talking About Food Preferences:**

```
A: What's your favorite food?
B: I really like Italian food. Pasta is my favorite.

A: What's it like?
B: It's salty. Sometimes a little spicy. But it's delicious.

A: Are you a fan of Mexican food?
B: I haven't tried it. What's it like?

A: Tacos are crispy and tasty. You should try them sometime.
B: Maybe next time. Let's have pasta today.
```
