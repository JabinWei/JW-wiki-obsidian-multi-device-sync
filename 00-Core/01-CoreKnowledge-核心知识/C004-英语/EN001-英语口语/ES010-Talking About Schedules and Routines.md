## 🎯 学习目标

- 用英语描述早晨例行公事（morning routine）
- 掌握时间表达：数字时间（7:30）、half past / quarter past 的切换
- 正确使用时间介词 at / in / on（at six o'clock, in the morning, on Tuesday）
- 使用频率副词描述多久做一次（sometimes, usually, always）
- 用 "What's your morning routine?" 询问他人日常

## 📚 核心内容

### 一、Morning Routine 早晨日常

```
wake up / get up      → 起床
have a cup of coffee  → 喝杯咖啡
have breakfast        → 吃早餐
check emails          → 查邮件
go to work            → 去上班
get to work           → 到达工作地点
have a meeting        → 开会
```

**描述你的 morning routine：**

```
What's your morning routine?
→ I usually get up at 7. First, I have a cup of coffee.
  Then I check my emails. I get to work around 9.
```

### 二、时间表达的两种方式

| 数字式（办公室常用） | 传统式 | 中文 |
|---------------------|--------|------|
| 7:30 | half past seven | 七点半 |
| 10:15 | quarter past ten | 十点一刻 |
| 6:00 | six o'clock | 六点 |
| 2:00 | two o'clock | 两点 |

> **老师说**：在办公室/工作场合，人们通常用数字式（7:30, 2:05），因为清晰明了。`half past seven` 或 `quarter past ten` 这种表达偏传统，有时非母语者不易理解。

### 三、时间介词 at / in / on 完整规则

| 介词 | 用于 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **at** | 具体时间点 | at six o'clock, at noon, at night |
| **in** | 月份/年份/一天中的时段 | in June, in 2026, in the morning |
| **on** | 具体日期/星期 | on Tuesday, on Sunday morning |

```
✅ at six o'clock      ✅ at noon          ✅ at night
✅ in the morning      ✅ in the afternoon  ✅ in the evening
✅ in June             ✅ in 2026
✅ on Tuesday          ✅ on Sunday morning  ✅ on weekends
```

> **易错点**：`in this week` ❌ → `this week` 不加介词（this/next/last 前面不用介词）

### 四、频率副词 (Frequency Adverbs)

| 频率 | 副词 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 100% | always | I always get up at 7. |
| 80% | usually | I usually check emails first. |
| 60% | often | I often have coffee. |
| 40% | sometimes | Sometimes I work late. |
| 20% | rarely | I rarely have meetings in the morning. |
| 0% | never | I never skip breakfast. |

**频率副词的位置**：
- 在 be 动词后：I am **usually** tired in the morning.
- 在实义动词前：I **always** check emails.
- sometimes 可放句首：**Sometimes** I work late.

### 五、Work Schedule 相关表达

```
9 to 5 job             → 朝九晚五
work overtime          → 加班
on call                → 随时待命
check emails           → 查邮件
read messages          → 读消息
finish work            → 完成工作
to-do list             → 待办清单
```

**对话示例：**

```
A: Do you have to be on call on the weekend?
B: Sometimes. I have to check my phone and answer messages.

A: What time do you usually finish work?
B: Usually around 7 p.m. Sometimes I work overtime until 9 or 10.
```

### 六、"have" 万能动词再强调

`have` 是非常常用的替代词，可替代 eat/drink/take：

```
have breakfast    代替 eat breakfast
have a coffee     代替 drink coffee
have a meeting    代替 attend a meeting
have a rest       代替 take a rest
```
