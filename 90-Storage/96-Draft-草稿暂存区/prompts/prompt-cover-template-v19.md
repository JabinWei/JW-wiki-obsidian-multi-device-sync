A reusable book/note cover template in gouache illustration style.

- Center: a large white rounded-corner document card (like a file/Preview icon), floating with soft subtle shadow
- Top-right corner of the card is folded down (classic page-fold effect), showing a soft underside shadow
- Middle of the card: a rounded-square "preview window" (white rounded frame inset into the card), inside which is a small gouache scene

**Scene inside preview window**:

**Loupe (key element)**:

**Below the card**: empty clean white space (leave blank for title text overlay later)
