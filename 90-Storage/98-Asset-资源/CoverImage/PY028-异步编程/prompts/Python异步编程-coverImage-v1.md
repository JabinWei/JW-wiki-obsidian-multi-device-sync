# Composition
Main visual: One single Python snake (deep navy) sits calmly at the center as a dispatcher/scheduler, wearing a small conductor's cap, in front of a control panel with several glowing task-indicator lights. From the snake, one elegant ribbon/line connects outward to several floating task bubbles arranged around it — each bubble holds an hourglass (representing tasks that are "waiting"). The snake is deliberately NOT cloned (that would be multiprocessing) and has no extra hands (that would be threading) — it is one single body with one head, coordinating many waiting tasks and switching its attention between them. Metaphor: asyncio as one single-threaded scheduler (统筹) managing multiple IO waits at once.

Background: Warm cream paper (#FAF5ED).
