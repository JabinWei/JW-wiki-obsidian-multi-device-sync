# Composition
Main visual: A hand-drawn 2×2 coordinate grid (a cross-axes map) centered on a warm cream paper background, like a page from a sketchbook. **CRITICAL: the four quadrants must be perfectly symmetric — identical icon size, identical color treatment, identical visual weight. NO quadrant is highlighted, enlarged, glowing, or given a halo, flag, or spotlight. This is a neutral taxonomy map, not an advertisement for any one language.**

Axes, drawn as slightly wobbly hand-sketched lines with arrows:
- Horizontal axis (bottom), labeled at each end: left "静态" (static), right "动态" (dynamic). A small arrow above the axis reads "检查时机" (when types are checked).
- Vertical axis (left), labeled at top "强类型" (strong), at bottom "弱类型" (weak). A small arrow beside the axis reads "是否隐式转换" (implicit conversion).
- The two axes cross at the center, drawn in deep navy.

Four quadrants, **visually identical in size and treatment**, each holding one simple hand-drawn language mascot icon with a small hand-lettered label beneath it. The icon is just a representative glyph, equal in scale in all four corners:
- TOP-LEFT quadrant (static + strong): a small steaming coffee cup icon (Java / C# / Go / Rust). Bold label: "静态强". A tiny muted footnote below: "Java · C# · Go".
- TOP-RIGHT quadrant (dynamic + strong): a small friendly hand-drawn snake icon (Python / Ruby). Bold label: "动态强". A tiny muted footnote below: "Python · Ruby". **Same size and color treatment as the other three icons — no coral halo, no flag, no glow.**
- BOTTOM-LEFT quadrant (static + weak): a small cog/gear icon (C / C++). Bold label: "静态弱". Tiny footnote: "C · C++".
- BOTTOM-RIGHT quadrant (dynamic + weak): a small lightning-bolt icon (JavaScript / PHP). Bold label: "动态弱". Tiny footnote: "JavaScript · PHP".

Metaphor: the cover is a "map" showing that static/dynamic and strong/weak are two INDEPENDENT axes you plot languages on — not a single "how good is the language" scale. The emphasis is entirely on the two perpendicular axes and the 2×2 grid structure, not on any one occupant.

Background: warm cream paper (#FAF5ED) with subtle paper grain, a few faint sketch hatching marks in corners, loose and breathable whitespace (40-60%).

Color: axes and outlines in deep navy. The four icons each get a single muted accent (coffee cup warm amber, snake soft teal-blue, gear teal, lightning muted gold) — all equally saturated, no single icon dominates. Hand-lettered quadrant labels in charcoal. Footnote language names in small light-gray handwriting. Title in deep navy.

Typography: Title "动静与强弱" in bouncy handwritten marker style, placed upper-left, medium-large scale, as the clear visual anchor.
